Q1.	Consider the following grammar G:
S → baB
B → abA
A → bB | aa

Construct a left-linear grammar for the language generated by grammar G.

S = baB
S = ba (abA)
S = ba (ab (bB | aa))

so grammar is either

S= baabaa
or 
S=baabbB
